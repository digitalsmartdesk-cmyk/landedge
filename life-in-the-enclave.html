Life in the enclave

Five families share an address, not a routine. Here is how privacy, security and service are managed.

Privacy

Each house stands apart, with trees, a wall or a water channel between plots, depending on the project. No two houses in Comfort face each other. In Premium, each house has its own private entry court.

A+ security

Every enclave is gated, with cameras and a guard or command room at the entrance. Comfort and Premium add a visitor lobby at the arrival court.

Staff and service lanes

Staff, caterers and deliveries use their own gate or lane in every project, kept apart from the family's entrance. In Premium, a green screen wall hides the back lane from the central court.

Celebrations, hosted at home

The ballroom and lawn are for the owner's family and their guests. They are not offered for hire to the public, and no paid events are hosted at the enclave.

The monthly charge

Owners pay a monthly charge that covers security, and the upkeep of shared grounds, buildings and staff. Figures are available on request.
