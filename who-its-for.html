Who a destination house is for

A LandEdge house is a home your family travels to, at the edge of a lake or a forest, within 50 km of Vadodara. It suits some families very well and others not at all. This page helps you see which you are.

The short answer

It is ideal if your family gathers in numbers, you like to host at home, you want real privacy from your neighbours, and you want the house looked after while you are away.

Six families it suits

Find yourself in one of these

Each house is about 6,000 to 6,500 sq ft over two storeys, with a ballroom for 50 guests, a dining room, a swimming pool and a lawn, in an enclave of five houses behind one gate.

The family that gathers

Three generations, cousins from other cities, and a festival that fills the house. Diwali, Navratri, a wedding in the family.

Everyone can be together under one roof, and nobody is squeezed. The ground-floor event wing seats up to 50 guests, with a dining room beside it. The plot is 2 to 2.5 bigha, so there is lawn for the overflow and parking for four cars at the house.

The family that likes to host

An engagement, an anniversary, a sangeet, a family garba. You would rather host at your own place than book a venue.

The event wing is part of the house, so the celebration is yours from the first guest to the last. In Lake Edge and Stepwell Court the caterers work from a separate kitchen annex with its own loading dock, so cooking and delivery stay away from your guests. In Stepwell Court, staff and caterers also use a back lane and never cross the lawn court. Forest Edge has a community kitchen by the gate.

The ballroom is for the owner's family only. It is never hired out to outside parties.

The weekender from the city

You live in Vadodara, Ahmedabad, Surat or Mumbai. You want Friday evening to feel far away without a long drive.

Every site is within 50 km of Vadodara. You arrive to a pool, a lawn and a lake or forest at the edge of the land. When you are not there, the enclave is gated with A+ security and a facilities office. Lake Edge and Stepwell Court also give each house its own staff cottage.

The family abroad that comes home

You live overseas and come back for a month in winter and for family occasions.

The house is looked after for the other eleven months by an enclave that is secured and staffed on site. Lake Edge and Stepwell Court have a main building with 10 guest rooms, so relatives who do not fit in the house stay close by. Security and upkeep are paid as a monthly charge. The family that values privacy

Your name is known and you prefer not to be seen. You want neighbours who are close enough to be safe and far enough to be invisible.

There are no shops and no through traffic. Every plot has its own wall. Houses are 6 m apart in Forest Edge, 10 m apart and staggered in Lake Edge so that no two face each other, and 15 m apart in Stepwell Court, with a tree belt and a water channel between them. In Stepwell Court every house also has its own entry court.

The family with children and elders

Grandparents who want a quiet walk, and children who want room to run.

The grounds have no through traffic. Lake Edge and Stepwell Court add a looping walking and cycling trail, a children's play area, a court for tennis or pickleball, a stargazing deck at the lake, and a concierge and medical room on site. Stepwell Court adds a garba lawn, a bird hide and an organic orchard and farm. Forest Edge keeps to the lawn, the pool and the wall.

Who it is not for

Five reasons to look elsewhere

It is better to know now than after a site visit.

You want rental or investment income

These are homes for a family. The ballroom is for the owner's family only, and the guest rooms are for the owner's visiting guests. Letting either to the public may count as commercial use of the land, so LandEdge does not offer them that way.

You want to live near the city

The sites are outside Vadodara, within 50 km. Expect a drive, and plan your weeks around it.

You want a bar, a club or a cellar

Gujarat is a dry state. There is no bar and no club in any of the enclaves.

You want hotel service

A facilities office and staff look after the enclave, but you own a home. It does not run like a hotel.

You want to design your own house

Each enclave has five identical houses, built by LandEdge. You choose the project, and the house comes as designed.
