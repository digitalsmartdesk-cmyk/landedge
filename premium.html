Privacy and security

Each house has its own private entry court. Houses stand 15 m apart, with a tree belt and a low water channel between plots. Staff, caterers and deliveries use a separate back lane, hidden by a green screen wall, and never cross the central court. The enclave has A+ security — gated, with cameras and round-the-clock management. A monthly charge from owners covers security and upkeep — details on request.
